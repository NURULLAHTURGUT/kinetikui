## Usage

```tsx
import { CompareSlider } from '@/components/kinetik/compare-slider';

<CompareSlider
  accessibilityLabel="Colour grade"
  beforeLabel="Original"
  afterLabel="Graded"
  before={<Image source={original} style={StyleSheet.absoluteFill} />}
  after={<Image source={graded} style={StyleSheet.absoluteFill} />}
/>;
```

`before` and `after` fill the frame, one over the other. The divider shows `before` to its left and `after` to its right.

## When to use

- Showing what an edit, a filter or a restoration changed.
- Before and after for renovations, cleaning, retouching, maps across years.

## When not to use

- Pictures that do not line up. Show them side by side.
- More than two versions. Use a carousel.

## Notes

- The frame takes the width it is given and sets its height with `aspectRatio` (4:3 by default). Make both pictures fill it.
- The `before` picture is cut by the divider, never squeezed.
- Drag anywhere on the picture, or tap to jump the divider there. Vertical drags still scroll the page.
- On first sight the divider nudges once to show that it moves; turn this off with `hint={false}`.
- With reduced motion there is no nudge, and jumps happen without a spring.
