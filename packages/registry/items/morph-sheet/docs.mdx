## Usage

```tsx
import { MorphSheet } from '@/components/kinetik/morph-sheet';

const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<MorphSheet
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  snapPoints={[0.5, 0.92]}
  accessibilityLabel="Share photo"
  sheet={<ShareOptions />}
>
  <PhotoScreen onShare={() => setOpen(true)} />
</MorphSheet>;
```

Wrap the screen in `MorphSheet` and pass the sheet's content as `sheet`. The sheet opens at `initialSnap`, settles at the nearest snap point when released (taking the flick into account) and closes when dragged or flicked below its lowest stop.

## When to use

- Secondary tasks over the current screen: share, filters, details, quick actions.
- Content with a short and an expanded form, such as a preview that can grow into a full view.

## When not to use

- Primary navigation or long flows. Push a screen instead.
- Content that needs its own vertical scrolling at the lowest stop; the drag and the scroll compete. Expand to the top stop first.

## Notes

- The screen behind is wrapped in a `BlurTargetView`, which Android needs for a real blur (`expo-blur`'s `blurMethod` falls back to a tint below Android 12).
- While open the sheet is modal for screen readers and offers close, expand and collapse actions; the dimmed scrim is a close button.
- A tick plays when the sheet settles at a new stop.
