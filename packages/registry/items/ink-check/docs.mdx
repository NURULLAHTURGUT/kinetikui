## Usage

```tsx
import { InkCheck } from '@/components/kinetik/ink-check';

<InkCheck
  label="Book the flights"
  description="Lisbon, 14 to 21 June"
  checked={booked}
  onCheckedChange={setBooked}
/>;
```

Set `strike` for to-do lists: the label is struck through and greys a little once it is checked.

## When to use

- Lists of independent choices: settings, filters, a to-do or packing list.
- Consent and agreement lines in forms.

## When not to use

- A setting that takes effect immediately. Use a switch, such as Pulse Toggle.
- Choosing one option from a set. Use Choice Cards or radio buttons.

## Notes

- The tick is drawn by two plain views rather than a canvas, so a screen with fifty checkboxes costs no more than fifty small views.
- The tick is drawn in `onAzure`. If you pass a light `color`, check the contrast of the tick against it.
- The label stays on one line so the strike-through can follow it; put detail in `description`.
- With reduced motion the box and tick change at once.
