## Usage

```tsx
import { ScanProgress } from '@/components/kinetik/scan-progress';

<ScanProgress
  source={{ uri: video.thumbnail }}
  imageLabel="Your swing, frame 1"
  progress={job.progress}
  done={job.status === 'complete'}
  label={job.stepLabel}
/>;
```

Leave out `progress` when the work has no measurable progress: the ring creeps towards 90% and slows down, and closes when `done` turns true.

## When to use

- Processing a photo or video the person just gave you: AI analysis, enhancement, background removal, upload checks.
- Waits of a few seconds to a minute, where seeing the image being worked on reassures.

## When not to use

- Instant results under a second: the effect would only flash.
- Waits without an image: use a progress bar or Ring Spinner.

## Notes

- `label` changes cross-fade, so you can name each step as it happens.
- The pixel field draws white squares over the image; pick a `tint` that suits your photos (it defaults to the theme success green).
- With reduced motion the pixel field and spinner rotation are left out; the ring and labels still report progress.
