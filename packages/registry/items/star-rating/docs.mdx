## Usage

```tsx
import { StarRating } from '@/components/kinetik/star-rating';

<StarRating accessibilityLabel="Your rating" value={rating} onValueChange={setRating} />

// Read-only, with half stars
<StarRating accessibilityLabel="Average rating" value={4.5} allowHalf size={14} />
```

Leave out `onValueChange` for a read-only rating. Tap a star to rate up to it, or drag across the row; tapping the current rating again clears it unless `clearable={false}`.

## When to use

- Asking for a quick rating after a stay, a ride, an order or an episode.
- Showing an average rating beside a listing, read-only.

## When not to use

- Scales that need words at each step (disagree to agree). Use labelled options.
- Precise scores. Use a stepper or a slider.

## Notes

- All the stars share one Skia canvas; the fill and the swell are driven by two shared values, so a rating in every row of a list stays light.
- With `allowHalf` the left half of a star rates a half.
- With reduced motion the stars fill at once.
